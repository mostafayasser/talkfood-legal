# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

TalkFood ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the TalkFood mobile application.

## Voice and food logging

TalkFood allows users to log food intake using voice or text input. When you speak into the app, your voice is converted into text using your device's built-in speech recognition functionality. TalkFood does not store, record, or retain voice audio.

The transcribed or typed text may be processed using third-party AI services, including the Gemini API, solely for the purpose of identifying food items and estimating nutritional information such as calories and protein. No personal identifiers are included in this processing.

## Data stored on your device

TalkFood works without an account. Unless you turn on cloud sync, your profile (such as your name, age, gender, height, weight and goals), food logs, saved meals, weight logs and progress photos are stored only on your device. Progress photos are encrypted on your device.

## Optional account and cloud sync

Cloud sync is off by default. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google and turn on sync in Settings → Account & Sync, TalkFood stores a copy of your data in your account so it stays the same on all your devices. This includes your profile, food logs, saved meals, weight logs and progress photos. Your account ID and the email address provided by Apple or Google are used to identify your account.

Synced data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Functions) in the United States. Progress photos are encrypted with AES-256 on your device before they are uploaded; the encryption key is stored with your account so your other devices can open them.

To keep your devices in sync, TalkFood registers a push token for each signed-in device and sends silent notifications (with no alerts) when your data changes on another device. Meals you log with Siri are uploaded to your account right away when sync is on.

You can turn sync off or sign out at any time in Settings → Account & Sync; your data then stays only on your device.

## Apple Health / HealthKit

On iOS, TalkFood may request permission to read your Active Energy Burned from Apple Health. If you grant permission, this information is used only to adjust your daily calorie target inside the app and on its Home Screen widget, including in the background while the app is closed.

Apple Health access is optional. You can grant, deny, or revoke access at any time in Apple Health or iOS Settings. TalkFood currently reads Active Energy Burned only and does not write data to Apple Health.

Apple Health data is processed on your device and is not uploaded to TalkFood servers, including when cloud sync is on. TalkFood does not sell, share, or use Apple Health data for advertising, marketing, tracking, or profiling.

## Analytics and crash reports

TalkFood collects anonymous usage analytics (through PostHog) to understand how features are used and improve the app. TalkFood also collects crash reports (through Firebase Crashlytics), which include device and app information and, if you are signed in, your account ID, so problems can be diagnosed and fixed.

TalkFood does not use this information for advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Subscriptions

TalkFood may offer optional in-app subscriptions. Payments are processed securely through the App Store or Google Play using RevenueCat. TalkFood does not collect or store payment information. If you are signed in, your account ID is used to restore your subscription on your other devices.

## Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account in Settings → Account & Sync → Delete account. This removes your account and all of your synced data from our servers and erases your data on that device. Data that exists only on your device is removed when you delete the app. You can also contact us to request deletion.

TalkFood does not sell your personal information.

## Children

TalkFood is not intended for use by children under the age of 13.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If new features require additional data handling, this policy will be updated accordingly.
